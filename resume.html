Why I'm different

I spent 19 years building quantitative research infrastructure inside institutional investment management — an environment where data quality is non-negotiable and analytical systems run in production every day. I didn't just analyze data. I built the pipelines, dashboards, factor models, and R tooling that made analysis possible at scale, with full audit trails and automated quality checks.

The stakes shaped how I think. In finance, a broken pipeline or a stale dataset doesn't just slow things down — it changes decisions that move real money. That environment gave me a production mindset before I ever heard the term. I now bring that discipline to data engineering full time, with modern tooling — Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, React — added on top of a foundation that most engineers spend years building.

Core strengths

  • Decision-first system design — repeatable, auditable workflows that serve the people making the call
  • Data pipelines: ingestion → normalization → validation → publish
  • Analytics tooling for analyst and PM workflows — dashboards that drive decisions, not just report on them
  • High-stakes data environments — bad data has real consequences and I design systems accordingly
  • Domain translation — bridging the gap between what the business needs and what the data can provide
  • Clear communication: what changed, why it matters, what to do next
